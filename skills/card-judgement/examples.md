# Example bank: rejected drafts and the versions approved instead

Real verdicts, de-identified. **✗** marks a rejected draft, **✓** the approved version. Cloze hints
appear as `{{c1::answer::hint}}`. A personal judgement skill may hold the user's own examples; prefer
those when they exist.

## Rule 1: Nothing visible gives the answer away

**Depo-Provera.**
✗ `Depo-Provera can delay return of fertility by up to {{c1::1 year::time}} after stopping – a poor choice for women wanting pregnancy soon.` The trailing clause is the clue.
✓ Front: `A woman wants to conceive within the next year. Which contraceptive method should she avoid?` Back: `Depo-Provera: return of fertility can be delayed by up to 1 year after the last injection.`

**Seizure aetiology.**
✗ `a seizure is either {{c1::acute symptomatic (provoked)::due to an acute brain insult}} or due to {{c2::epilepsy::enduring predisposition}}`. Both hints define the answer.
✓ Front: `Seizures: what are the 2 broad aetiological categories?`

**Infant pulse check.**
✗ `…an 8-month-old is unresponsive. Which two pulses do you feel, and why not the carotid?` "Not the carotid" gives away the answer, and the Front asks two questions.

**Naming a retrieval step.**
✗ `…biopsy shows Reed–Sternberg cells. Which Hodgkin lymphoma subtype…?`
✓ `…biopsy shows Reed–Sternberg cells. Which disease subtype…?` The finding should cue the disease; don't name it.

## Rule 2: Hints state the answer type only

✗ `{{c3::PCB, IMB, PMB::bleeding ×3}} … {{c5::dyspareunia::sex}}`. Each hint is the answer.
✓ Basic: `Which 3 types of abnormal vaginal bleeding do you specifically ask about?`

✗ `…the social history must establish baseline function: {{c1::mobility, exercise tolerance, ADLs::3 areas}}`. The hint is fine, but the stem label summarises the answer.

✗ `the thiazolidinedione {{c1::pioglitazone::drug}} and the DPP-4 inhibitor {{c2::linagliptin::drug}}…`. Naming the class narrows each answer to one drug.

## Rule 3: Retrieve the reasoning

✗ `Heavy AND painful periods, planning pregnancy (so hormonal options are unsuitable): use {{c1::an NSAID::drug class}}`
✓ Front: `Heavy AND painful periods, planning to conceive soon. Most appropriate drug?` Back: `An NSAID, e.g. mefenamic acid.`
✓ `tranexamic acid reduces {{c1::bleeding only}}; NSAIDs reduce {{c1::both bleeding and pain}}`. The dichotomy shares one number.

## Rule 4: No solving by elimination

✗ Garden grades I–IV as c1…c4 on one note: three visible grades reveal the fourth.
✓ One note per grade, plus separate Basic cards: `Undisplaced intracapsular fracture: which operation?` and `Displaced…: which operation?`

✗ `{{c1::linagliptin::drug}} needs no dose adjustment, whereas sitagliptin needs a reduction…`. Naming the sibling gives it away.
✓ `Sitagliptin in CKD: reduce the dose once eGFR falls below {{c1::45::eGFR}}.` Linagliptin gets its own card.

## Rule 5: Extra is not storage

✗ A two-cloze nephrotic syndrome card with the secondary-cause bloods and the biopsy rationale parked in Extra.
✓ Seven lean notes, each testing one of those facts.

✗ `Femoral head blood supply: mainly the {{c1::medial circumflex femoral artery}}, via retinacular branches up the femoral neck.` The trailing detail makes the cloze easier, so it moves to Extra.

## Rule 6: Test the knowledge directly

✗ `A patient on chemotherapy has a temperature of 38.9 °C and neutrophils 0.35 × 10⁹/L two days ago. Cultures are taken. What do you start, and what about G-CSF?`
✓ Front: `First-line antibiotic for neutropenic sepsis?` Back: `IV piperacillin–tazobactam, started immediately.`
✓ `{{c1::Genetic testing for the dystrophin gene::investigation}} is the definitive test for {{c2::Duchenne muscular dystrophy::condition}}`

## Rule 10: Card only what is needed

Pieces learners cut: an amiodarone infusion time; codeine-per-day arithmetic; a legacy protocol kept
only "because textbooks quote it"; "list the four steroid potency classes"; "why are contraceptives
unsuitable when trying to conceive?".

## Rule 11: Verify

✗ `Childhood ALL maintenance: girls ~2 years, boys ~3 years`. Outdated.
✓ `Childhood ALL, current UK protocol: about 2 years in total, the same for boys and girls.`
